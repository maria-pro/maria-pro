# Maria Prokofieva

I build machine-learning and language-model methods that turn what people write and do into evidence that can be checked.

I apply them where judgement carries consequences:

- **Accounting, audit and financial reporting.** [finagent](https://github.com/maria-pro/finagent): grounded multi-agent equity research on a small NVIDIA Nemotron model. Specialist verifiers for agent governance ([code](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23999276), [results](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23999280)). Managerial AI optimism in US technology earnings calls ([dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23795230)).
- **Digital behaviour.** Machine learning of digital traces to detect risk for behavioural addictions online ([Nature Reviews Psychology, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-026-00604-8)). [cohort-harmonise](https://github.com/maria-pro/cohort-harmonise): dictionary-first harmonisation and claim auditing across five youth-development cohorts.
- **Education.** [Large language models in the social sciences](https://github.com/maria-pro/LLM_SS): course materials. Research on teaching analytics and AI in accounting.

Measurement underneath all three: [esem](https://github.com/maria-pro/esem), an R package for exploratory structural equation modeling on CRAN.
